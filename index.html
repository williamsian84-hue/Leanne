About Leanne

Leanne is a warm, judgement-free AI style and confidence companion — built for anyone exploring, refining, or rebuilding their personal style, at their own pace, on their own terms.

At its heart, Leanne is a conversation. Take a photo, and Leanne suggests real outfits built from your own wardrobe or new pieces worth adding, shown on you rather than described in the abstract. Every suggested item is a single tap away from the retailer that sells it — no hunting through search results, no guessing whether something will actually work with what you already own.

Leanne's Wardrobe keeps track of what you have, organises it by season and occasion, and quietly notices what's missing rather than pushing you to buy things you don't need. The interactive Mirror lets you experiment freely with hair, makeup, and styling choices with no pressure and nothing saved unless you choose to keep it. Dream Board is a private place to collect the looks, pieces, and ideas you're working towards. The Shopping and Holiday Planner turns a wish list into a real, budgeted plan — right down to a full weekend itinerary for our VIP members.

Underneath all of this sits Leanne Academy: 113 lessons across eight courses — makeup, grooming, hair and wigs, voice, movement, hair removal, clothing and styling, and confidence — written to be genuinely useful from your very first lesson, with no jargon and no assumptions about who you are or why you're here.

Leanne is built on a simple belief: confidence isn't something you're handed, it's something you build, one small, supported step at a time. Whether you're preparing for your first full look, planning your first outing, or just figuring out what you actually like, Leanne is there to help — warmly, practically, and entirely without judgement.

Leanne is free to start, with Plus and VIP tiers available for those who want a deeper wardrobe of AI styling, richer learning content, and more from the app as they grow into it. Every shopping link in Leanne, on every tier, works the same way — because we believe useful features shouldn't disappear behind a paywall, only the AI depth around them should.

4. Device-linked free tier and abuse prevention

To keep the free tier fair for everyone and to protect our cloud infrastructure costs, we link free-tier usage allowances to your physical device as well as your registered user account.

The tracking mechanism. For this purpose, the app uses a privacy-compliant, non-permanent device identifier known as an Identifier for Vendors (IDFV). We do not collect or store permanent hardware identifiers such as IMEI or MAC addresses.

Enforcement rules. If a device's free allowance has already been used, creating a new account on that same device will not receive a new free allowance. Access to the free tier on that device remains restricted unless the original account is signed back into, or a paid subscription is active.

Subscription cancellation. If a paid subscription is later cancelled, the account and associated device return to standard free-tier eligibility, subject to our fair-use limits.

We request zero data retention and disallow use of your data for provider model training on every request where this is supported. 6A. The one part of our data path we could not independently verify

When you use Leanne's AI features — such as sending styling messages or uploading photos for outfit analysis — your data passes through the following path: this app → our backend infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers and secure storage) → our development platform's own infrastructure proxy → our AI gateway → the AI model provider (Anthropic or Google).

We have independently verified and configured strong protections at every hop under our direct control, including enforceable zero data retention (ZDR) configurations on both our AI gateway and the AI model providers themselves, so that your queries and photos are not retained or used for model training at these points.

The one exception: our development platform's own infrastructure proxy, sitting in the middle of this path, forwards your data correctly and without alteration — we have verified this — but its own background logging or retention practices have not been independently confirmed to us in writing at the time of writing.

Our commitment: we have no operational reason to believe your data is exposed or stored inappropriately at this hop. We remain engaged with our platform provider to clarify this point, and we will update this policy — and require renewed consent — the moment this status changes.

7. Data retention and account deletion

We retain your personal data and User Content only for as long as your account remains active. You can delete your account at any time directly through the app's settings. Upon confirming your account deletion:

  • Immediate production purge: your wardrobe records, Dream Board configurations, style profile, saved looks, uploaded photos, and usage history are permanently deleted from our active production systems immediately.
  • Backup lifecycle: encrypted server backups containing this data (retained strictly for disaster recovery and system integrity) are automatically and permanently purged after a limited period. We are confirming the exact retention window with our infrastructure provider and will state the precise figure here once verified, rather than publish an unconfirmed number.
  • De-identified abuse prevention: a non-personal record linked to your device identifier (IDFV) is retained to enforce our fair-use free-tier policy and prevent repeated free-tier abuse. This record contains no personal information and cannot be used to identify you or reconstruct your account.
  • Authentication tokens: the underlying sign-in credential link with our authentication provider (Rork Auth) may not be independently deletable via an available API at this time. It grants no access to any data once your account has been deleted, and signing in again afterwards creates a new, empty account.
  • Statutory obligations: some records (such as billing history) may be retained for longer where we are legally required to do so.
